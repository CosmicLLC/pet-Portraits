# Paw Masterpiece — Social Media Operating Plan

_Living reference. Solo-founder-sized. Built on the fixed $6 funnel, the reveal mechanic, and the viral themes (Renaissance, Astronaut, DOGUE)._

## Strategy in one line
Win on **visual + emotional + giftable**. The **"photo → portrait reveal"** is the viral engine. Every post funnels to the **free 30-second preview → $6 digital → $79 framed print**.

## Platform stack (where the hours go)
| Platform | Role | Cadence | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **TikTok** | Cold viral reach (reveals) | 1 reveal/day | 🥇 |
| **Pinterest** | Search + gift discovery, compounding | 3–5 pins/day (scheduled) | 🥇 |
| **Instagram** (Reels + carousels + Stories) | Discovery + legit home base | 1 reel/day · 2–3 carousels/wk · daily Story | 🥈 |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Free long-tail repurpose | cross-post the reveal | 🥉 |
| **Facebook** (pet groups + page) | Warm/community = fastest first sales | a few group posts/wk | 🥉 |
| X / LinkedIn | Low fit | skip | — |

## Content pillars (the rotation)
- **Reveal (~40%)** — transformation, beat-drop, trending audio. The workhorse.
- **Theme showcase (~20%)** — Renaissance/royal, Astronaut, DOGUE, "rate my dog in 4 styles." Funny + shareable.
- **Gift angle (~15%)** — "the gift for the dog who has everything"; occasion-framed (Father's Day → Christmas → Valentine's...).
- **Memorial (~10%)** — separate, gentle tone. High-emotion, never salesy.
- **Educational / BTS (~10%)** — the 30-second process, photo tips.
- **UGC / Wall of Love (~5%, grows)** — real customer reactions.

## The content engine (what makes it sustainable)
**Batch once a week** (one 2–3 hr session): generate 8–12 portraits across styles/themes/breeds → assemble reveals → schedule the week. Then **1 clip → 4 platforms** (TikTok + Reels + Shorts + Pinterest video pin; strip the TikTok watermark before reposting). One batch = a full week of multi-platform presence.

## A typical week
- **Mon** reveal/motivation · **Tue** theme showcase (royal/astronaut/DOGUE) · **Wed** UGC/reaction · **Thu** how-it-works/tips · **Fri** gift guide · **Sat/Sun** best-performer repost + a gentle memorial post
- **Every day:** reply to *every* comment, pin a link comment, post a Story with the **link sticker → free preview**.

## The funnel (social → revenue)
Bio link → **free 30-second preview** (lowest-friction CTA) → **$6 digital** (impulse) → **$79 framed** upsell + email capture.
- Bio: `🎨 See your pet as art — free 30-sec preview 👇 [link]`
- In-video/caption CTA: **"Try it free, no sign-up — link in bio."**
- Pin a comment with the link; reply to every "how?/where?" comment (boosts reach *and* converts).

## Hook bank (reuse + remix)
1. "POV: your dog just found out he's actually 17th-century royalty."
2. "I turned my dog into [a Renaissance king / an astronaut / a DOGUE cover model] and I can't stop laughing."
3. "Rating my dog in [N] styles — which one wins?"
4. "Tell me you're a dog mom/dad without telling me." → reveal portrait above the couch.
5. "The gift for the dog [dad/mom] who has everything."
6. "Your pet → 30 seconds → a museum painting. I'll wait."
7. _(Memorial, gentle):_ "A quiet way to keep him on the wall."

## 30-day launch sequence
- **Week 1:** Lock profiles to `@pawmasterpiece` + bio link. Generate 10 reveals. Post 1/day. **Ride Father's Day** (see the campaign doc). Post the 30 Pinterest pins (spaced 3–5/day). **Verify the $6 funnel** before driving hard.
- **Week 2:** Double down on whatever earned *saves/shares*. Seed 3–5 pet micro-influencers (free framed print for a reveal). Turn on email capture.
- **Week 3–4:** Launch referral (after first sales), start the Wall of Love, keep batching. Everything is now **Q4 prep** (Q4 = 40–50% of the year).

## Metrics (weekly, ~5 min)
Track **saves + shares + profile clicks + link clicks** (not likes), and the **$6 → $79 take-rate**. Whichever *theme/format* wins → make three more of it.

## Minimum viable (30 min/day)
1 reveal cross-posted to TikTok + Reels + Shorts · schedule 3 Pinterest pins · reply to comments. That alone compounds.

## Don't
- Spread thin across every platform — TikTok + Pinterest + IG first.
- Wait on Meta reinstatement; treat it as gone.
- Blast all 30 pins in 10 minutes (spam signal) — schedule 3–5/day.
- Mix the gentle memorial tone with the funny/royal posting rhythm.
