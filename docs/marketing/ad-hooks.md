# Paw Masterpiece — 20 Ad Creative Hooks

**Use:** Meta (Facebook/Instagram) + TikTok. 9:16 vertical, UGC-style, iPhone-shootable.
**Date drafted:** 2026-05-24
**Distribution:** 6 POV/reveal · 5 Before/after · 5 Speed-flex · 4 Memorial (retargeting only)

Notes for the shooter:
- Vertical phone footage, natural light, no studio gear.
- Spoken-word voiceover, conversational pacing. 15–20 seconds total per video.
- When citing customer reactions, swap `[real customer quote here]` with a verified review before publishing.
- Memorial creatives are gated to retargeting and memorial-lookalike audiences. Do NOT run cold.

---

## POV / Reveal Hooks

### Hook 1 — POV reveal
- **Opening line:** "POV: my golden retriever just became Renaissance royalty."
- **Body copy:**
  - I uploaded one photo of Biscuit on a Tuesday.
  - Picked the Renaissance style because, obviously.
  - Thirty seconds later he's wearing a velvet cloak.
  - I will not be taking questions.
- **CTA:** Make yours a king at pawmasterpiece.com.
- **Visual direction:** Phone-held shot of the pet on a couch, then quick cut to phone screen showing the Renaissance result, then framed canvas leaned against a wall. Owner mock-bows to the dog.
- **Platform fit:** Both (TikTok-first, repurpose to Reels).
- **Audience targeting:** Broad, dog moms 25–45.
- **Compliance note:** Disclose AI-assisted in caption ("AI-assisted, human-curated styles"). Avoid claiming the dog "is" royalty in a way that misrepresents the product.

### Hook 2 — POV reveal
- **Opening line:** "POV: you let an AI paint your cat and it took her too seriously."
- **Body copy:**
  - This is Olive. She judges me daily.
  - I asked for an oil painting. One photo, one tap.
  - It came back like a 17th-century portrait of a small disappointed countess.
  - I have never felt more seen by my own cat.
- **CTA:** Try the oil painting style — free preview at pawmasterpiece.com.
- **Visual direction:** Cat sitting regally on a windowsill, slow zoom. Cut to phone showing the oil painting preview. End on the cat unimpressed.
- **Platform fit:** Both. Strong TikTok.
- **Audience targeting:** Cat lovers, 22–40.
- **Compliance note:** Mention "free preview, watermarked until purchase" in caption to align with FTC clarity on free-to-try claims.

## Speed-Flex Hooks

### Hook 12 — Speed flex
- **Opening line:** "I uploaded my dog thirty seconds ago. Here's what I got back."
- **Body copy:**
  - One photo. One tap.
  - No "wait 5 to 7 business days."
  - No "your artist is reviewing."
  - Just my dog, on a canvas, by Friday.
- **CTA:** Thirty seconds to try at pawmasterpiece.com.
- **Visual direction:** Real-time screen recording of the upload-to-preview flow on the phone, then a cut to the framed canvas on the wall.
- **Platform fit:** Both.
- **Audience targeting:** Broad, retargeting from competitor-interest audiences.
- **Compliance note:** Do not name Crown & Paw or West & Willow. Quoted competitor language ("wait 5 to 7 business days") must stay generic and not be presented as a direct quote from a specific brand.

### Hook 13 — Speed flex
- **Opening line:** "Other pet portrait sites: send us your photo, we'll get back to you in a week."

  - Pick a style.

- **Platform fit:** Meta-first (claim-style copy reads better there).
- **Audience targeting:** Competitor-interest audiences, retargeting.
- **Compliance note:** Generic "other pet portrait sites" only — no brand names. Pricing ($6 digital) must match site exactly.

## Memorial / Retargeting Hooks

**Gate:** Retargeting audiences and memorial-lookalike only. Never cold. Sober tone throughout. No promises about presence, eternity, or watching over.

### Hook 17 — Memorial (retargeting only)
- **Opening line:** "This is the only photo I have of him on the porch."
- **Body copy:**
  - It's a phone shot. Half-blurry.
  - I uploaded it last month.
  - Now there's a watercolor of him on the wall by the door.
  - It's a way to remember him.
- **CTA:** A quiet way to remember a pet. pawmasterpiece.com.
- **Visual direction:** Slow handheld of the framed watercolor by a front door. No dramatic music. Natural light. Owner's voiceover only — no on-camera reaction.
- **Platform fit:** Meta only (better targeting controls).
- **Audience targeting:** Retargeting only — site visitors who viewed memorial-style pages, memorial-lookalike.
- **Compliance note:** No "live forever," "watching over," "always with you" language. Avoid any health/loss promise. Keep factual.

### Hook 18 — Memorial (retargeting only)
- **Opening line:** "My grandma still has every photo of her cat on the fridge."
- **Body copy:**
  - I made her a line art portrait from the best one.
  - She framed it in the hallway.
  - It's small. It's quiet.
  - She liked having something she could hang up.
- **CTA:** A small thing to hang up. pawmasterpiece.com.
- **Visual direction:** A framed line art piece in a hallway, hung at eye level. Natural light. No close-up of grandma's face — respect for the moment. Maybe her hand adjusting the frame.
- **Platform fit:** Meta only.
- **Audience targeting:** Retargeting, gift-giver lookalike with memorial intent signals.
- **Compliance note:** Avoid framing as bereavement therapy or grief solution. Factual gift framing only.

### Hook 19 — Memorial (retargeting only)
- **Opening line:** "I didn't want a big thing. I wanted one good picture."
- **Body copy:**
  - The phone shots blur over the years.

- **Platform fit:** Meta only.
- **Audience targeting:** Retargeting from memorial page visitors only.
- **Compliance note:** Do not mention loss directly in the ad voiceover — context lives in the visual and the audience targeting. No "rainbow bridge" or eternity language.

### Hook 20 — Memorial (retargeting only)
- **Opening line:** "I asked for a portrait, not a goodbye."
- **Body copy:**
  - One photo from when he was three.
  - Oil painting style.
  - Framed, on a shelf with the other pictures.
  - He's just part of the room now.
- **CTA:** A way to remember a pet. pawmasterpiece.com.

- **Platform fit:** Meta only.
- **Audience targeting:** Retargeting only — past purchasers, memorial-lookalike, viewers of memorial gallery.
- **Compliance note:** "Way to remember" is the strongest emotional claim allowed. No promises of comfort, healing, or presence. Keep all framing observational.
